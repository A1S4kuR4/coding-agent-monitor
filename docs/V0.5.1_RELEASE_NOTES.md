## Changes

- The dashboard no longer shows coverage warnings for recoverable records
  skipped during an otherwise successful collection. This includes the main
  banner, 30-day metadata and selected-day detail. The diagnostic information
  remains in the Rust/TypeScript usage summary. A successful total can include
  only the accepted records and therefore be lower than the complete local
  usage; the dashboard does not call out that case. Collection failures and
  stale-data notices retain their existing behavior.
- The bundled offline pricing snapshots have been refreshed and expanded:
  LiteLLM from 3,039 to 4,509 entries and models.dev from 2,275 to 3,164.
  Previous-only model IDs remain available for older logs. The ccusage parser
  remains at v20.0.20. Source commits, checksums and maintenance rules are in
  the [pricing record](PRICING_MAINTENANCE.md) and
  [pricing manifest](../src-tauri/vendor/ccusage/pricing/pricing-manifest.json).

Cost figures remain **estimates at bundled public API reference rates**, not
provider bills, subscription charges or credit balances. Recalculating older
usage with this release may change its estimated cost. Models without a
confirmed price, including some internal Antigravity variants, still show cost
as unavailable; the larger tables do not provide complete model coverage.
No pricing lookup occurs at runtime.

## Distribution and verification

Target platform: Windows 11 x64. Both candidate installers are **unsigned**;
Windows may show a SmartScreen or unknown-publisher warning. Check the final
published SHA-256 checksum before use. The exercised NSIS and MSI install,
upgrade and uninstall lifecycles, plus a scoped native GUI check, passed with
isolated synthetic data. The MSI reused a remembered directory despite an
explicit test path on two attempts. This additional path-control check
**failed twice**; existing files were backed up and restored byte-for-byte.

The following v0.5.1 scenarios remain **NOT RUN**: tray menu Refresh/Open/Exit;
native 125%/150% DPI, 200% text size, theme restart and reduced motion;
developer-tools-free offline clean host; real logon/reboot and sleep/resume;
and a full GUI cycle under a real non-ASCII Windows profile. These are
disclosed coverage limits, not technical passes or inherited v0.5.0 waivers.
See the [verification record](V0.5.1_RELEASE_VERIFICATION.md) for exact evidence.
