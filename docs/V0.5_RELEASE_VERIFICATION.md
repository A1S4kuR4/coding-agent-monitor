## Manual walkthrough (2026-09-23)

Method: the existing `src-tauri/target/release/coding-agent-monitor.exe`
(built 2026-09-20 22:03 with the 22:02 frontend assets) ran on Windows 11 x64
against an **isolated synthetic profile** with Claude and Codex session data.
The 2026-09-23 `pnpm build` produced byte-identical frontend asset hashes; the
EXE was **not rebuilt**, so uncommitted collector work was not incorporated.
WebView2 was driven through CDP `:9224` at the native host's 125% DPI
(`window.devicePixelRatio=1.25`). Screenshots are ignored local evidence under
`.scaffold-root/v05-verify/shots/`: `01–12` were recaptured on 2026-09-23;
`20–32` cover the expanded cases. The isolated records and every screenshot
are synthetic evidence, never actual user usage.

Raw driver outputs were retained in `.scaffold-root/v05-verify/`:
`driver-wrapup-2026-09-23.log` (first attempt stopped at the absent Codex tab
because the original seed contained only Claude),
`driver-wrapup-2026-09-23-attempt2.log` through `-attempt7.log`, and
`driver-2026-09-23.log` plus `-attempt2.log`. The final wrap-up attempt had
**one FAIL (D4)** and explicitly reported E6 **NOT RUN**. The core driver had
**one assertion FAIL**: computed `transition-duration=1e-05s`, numerically
equal to its expected `0.01ms`; the reduced-motion rule also passed a direct
animation-iteration probe in the wrap-up driver. Earlier wrap-up FAILs were
harness assumptions corrected and retained in those logs: absent Codex seed,
125% DPI border rounding (`1.6px` CSS computes to 2 device pixels), selecting
the date child instead of the full week panel, counting bar tracks as colored
segments, probing the historical panel for today's short cost mark, probing
the wrong explainer, and sampling a brief refresh after it had settled.
